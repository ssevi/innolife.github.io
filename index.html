The problem we're solving

Gaps in cognitive, motor, communication and other areas of development are frequently missed in the early years. Existing screening is often limited in reach and detail, and rarely built with children with disabilities in mind.

We built Vidyagni so that screening can happen early, in the classroom, and lead straight to the right support.

How it works

From first screening to daily support, in one platform your teachers can use.

  1. Screen

    QuickScreener picks up early signals across five developmental domains.

  2. Profile

    Children who need a closer look get the full 90-item assessment. Urgent items are escalated for professional evaluation.

  3. Support

    Results link directly to flashcards, worksheets, activity cards and storybooks matched to each child's needs.

Tested in the field

About Innolife

Innolife Technologies is a health and education technology company focused on early childhood development.

We build tools that help schools screen for developmental and learning needs early, and support children with the right resources from the start. Our flagship platform, Vidyagni, brings structured screening and foundational learning together, and Innolife was set up to take it to schools across India.

Our mission

To make reliable, early developmental screening and foundational learning support accessible to every child in India, starting in the classroom.

Our vision

A country where no child's developmental or learning need goes unnoticed, and where screening is routine, early, and built into how schools already work.
